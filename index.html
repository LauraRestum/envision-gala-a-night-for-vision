      In the Beren Room

      Innovative Assistive Technology Experience

      Presented by the Envision Vision Rehabilitation Center

      Guests experienced Meta smart glasses through a guided demonstration led by Envision's clinical team, and saw how emerging assistive technologies are expanding access, independence and possibility for people who are blind or have low vision.

      Tactile Art Experience

      Presented by Envision Arts

      A curated selection of tactile works from the Envision Arts team, inviting guests to experience each piece through touch.

      Experiences sponsored by High Touch Technologies, Forvis Mazars, 6 Meridian, Hutton, IMA and Summit Films.

      To improve the quality of life and provide inspiration and opportunity for people who are blind or have low vision through employment, outreach, rehabilitation, education and research.
